# Glide (Mac)

Glide is a note-taking and planning app for Mac, built for high school and college students. Students can add their tasks, such as homework, along with their deadlines. Glide can then track them and lay them out on a timeline. Students can also write notes in the text editor so all their notes stay in one place. Every note is saved as a Markdown file in a Glide folder on your computer so that you can open it in any text editor. You add a task by typing [ ] at the start of a line. Then you can add a time, like 3:30 PM, put the task name before it, and it will show up on the timeline. The timeline on the left side of the screen collects tasks from a single note or from all your notes. It sorts them by today, this week, or this month, and it has sections for what is up next, what is overdue, and what has no due date. We also have a mini window that sits in the menu bar at the top of your screen, even when you are in different apps, so you can quickly glance at your tasks or add to your tasks or notes without switching apps.

### Future Features

- Notifications for tasks
- Better text formatting options
- Ability to drag tasks in the timeline
- iCloud/Google Drive sync
- Calendar integration (Google Calendar and Apple Calendar)
- A context aware homework planner that can determine how long tasks will take
- Auto task splitter (break down large tasks into smaller tasks that; ex: split the work of writing an essay into multiple days)
- Brain-dump (user writes everything they know about a topic and the app formats and organizes the content)
- Note dictation
- Customization
- An IOS version of the app
- Website version of the app for school Chromebooks
- And more!!!
